# Priority source givens and auxiliary constructions (91-source shortlist)

This file is a 91-source priority shortlist (63 grade 8 and 28 grade 9), not the exhaustive inventory. The full audit covers every available grade 6-9 problem manifest. It retains 821 geometry records: 366 from grades 6-7, 212 from grade 8, and 243 from grade 9. Of these, 792 are `prepared` and 29 are `needs_review`. It excludes 9 A04-only grade 6-7 records, 23 algebra-focused grade 8 records, and 32 algebra or non-geometry grade 9 records; the mixed A04/G03 right-triangle construction is retained. The repository has no grade 10-12 problem manifest. `source-batches.jsonl` groups the 821 retained records by book into batches of 20 with partial final batches.

The exhaustive retained-source inventories are `givens-grade6-sgk1.md`, `givens-grade6-sgk2.md`, `givens-grade7-sgk1.md`, `givens-grade7-sgk2.md`, `givens-grade8-sgk1.md`, `givens-grade8-sgk2.md`, `givens-grade9-sgk1.md`, and `givens-grade9-sgk2.md`. They distinguish statement givens, crop-dependent facts, solution-added auxiliary constructions, and proof conclusions. Rows marked `needs_review` stay identified for review and are not treated as ready examples.

## 3,000-variant policy

Keep all variants of a source family together for split assignment. Generate variants only from dependency-closed subsets of source givens and verified source-derived constructions; do not count renaming, arbitrary layout changes, or raw set combinations as mathematical diversity. The construction graph above supports multiple variants (e.g. three independent median-pair scopes, staged secant/midpoint constructions, or one/both secondary altitudes), but the number of valid target pairs is not established by a combinatorial estimate. Every instruction still needs its own complete target TikZ and review evidence before it is a dataset sample. This audit is a source inventory, not 3,000 completed training pairs.
